### 4. Quality Standard
- Confirm the standard and proofreading steps

### 5. Process and Revisions
- Confirm the quote, workflow and revision policy

After the order is confirmed, a linguist is matched to the subject area, often working with CAT tools such as Trados and MemoQ. The translator edits and proofreads, a second qualified translator checks the work, and the project manager runs final quality assurance before delivery.
